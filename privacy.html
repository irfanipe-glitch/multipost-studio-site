Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Operator: Irfan · Contact: GitHub Issues

1. Summary

MultiPost Studio runs locally on your Windows computer. The operator does not operate a server that collects your videos, captions or account tokens. This website is a static informational page with no analytics or advertising trackers.

2. Information stored on your computer

  • Videos: files you choose to upload are stored in a local folder on your computer.
  • Settings and post history: stored in a local SQLite database.
  • OAuth tokens: access and refresh tokens are encrypted with a local key and stored in that database. They are never sent to the operator.
  • Browser data: a temporary CSRF cookie is used to protect the local dashboard, and a theme preference may be kept in browser local storage.

3. Information sent to social platforms

When you explicitly confirm a publish action, the selected video, caption, hashtags and settings are sent directly from your computer to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook using the official API of that platform. Those platforms then handle the data under their own privacy policies.

4. Who has access

Only you and anyone with access to your Windows user account can access the local files. The operator cannot see your local database or videos unless you deliberately send them.

5. This website

The static site at this address contains only documentation and legal notices. It does not set advertising cookies and does not collect form submissions. Hosting may collect basic server logs such as IP addresses, as is standard for static hosting providers.

6. Your choices

You can delete videos from the local Media Library, disconnect accounts, or delete the application's local data folder at any time. Deleting OAuth tokens requires reconnecting accounts before further publishing.

7. Contact

Privacy questions can be posted at GitHub Issues.
